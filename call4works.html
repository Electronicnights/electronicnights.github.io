Call for Multichannel Works

Electronic Nights No. 8, 9, & 10
The School of Music at Texas Tech University invites composers and intermedia artists to submit multichannel electroacoustic works (4-channel). For this cycle, we are accepting works for both audio-only and audio-visual (video) formats.

📥 Submission Materials

  • Four Mono Audio Files: 4-channel stems labeled (FL, FR, RL, RR).
  • Video File (Optional): For audio-visual submissions.
  • Channel Layout Plan: Diagram or text file for spatial arrangement.
  • Stereo Reference: One stereo MP3 (320 kbps).
  • Word File: Containing a 150-word bio and 150-word Program Note.
  • Headshot: High-resolution (JPEG/PNG).

⚙️ Technical Specifications

Quad Delivery: 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV preferred. Filenames: Lastname_Title_CH1.wav...
Video: .mp4 or .mov (1080p or 4K).
Storage: Google Drive or Dropbox link. Must be active until the end of 2026.

🗓️ Important Dates

Deadline: August 20, 2026
Link Expiry: Must remain active through December 31, 2026.

Email your link to: call4electronicworks@gmail.com
